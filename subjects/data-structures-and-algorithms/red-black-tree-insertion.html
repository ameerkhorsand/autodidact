Red-black tree insertion

Insert has two phases. First, insert the key as in a normal BST and color the new node red. Second, fix the colors with recoloring and rotations. The fix has four cases: the root, a red uncle, a black uncle with a bend, and a black uncle with a straight line. Choose an example and watch the colors and links change.
