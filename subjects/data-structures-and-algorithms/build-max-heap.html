Build-Max-Heap

Build-Max-Heap turns the array 5, 3, 17, 10, 84, 19, 6, 22, 9 into a max-heap. The nodes 5 to 9 are leaves, and a leaf is already a max-heap. The loop calls Max-Heapify on the other nodes, from node 4 up to node 1. So both subtrees of a node are max-heaps before the node is fixed. Step through the run and watch the call stack. The array uses the positions 1 to 9. The children of node i are 2i and 2i + 1.
