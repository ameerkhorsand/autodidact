Count the insertion orders that build a BST

One binary search tree can come from many insertion orders. The root must be first. After it, the keys of the left subtree and the keys of the right subtree can mix in any way. So T(n) = T(n1) · T(n2) · (n1 + n2)! / (n1! · n2!). Choose a tree and watch the recursion count the orders.
